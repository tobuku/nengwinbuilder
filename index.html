About Us

Built on Dedication, Driven by Quality

"Honesty, Integrity and Quality Workmanship"

Neng Win Builder Ltd has proudly served Honolulu and the island of Oahu since 2012. Our mission is simple: provide quality construction services at an affordable price.

The success of our company comes from the dedication we provide to every customer. Customer satisfaction is always our number one priority, and we take pride in treating every project -- whether large or small -- with the same level of care and professionalism.

From 2014 to 2020, we partnered with Win Win Builder, LLC, completing numerous residential and commercial projects across the island. Today, we continue to build on that experience and reputation for excellence.
